Privacy Policy

Guess the Career · Last updated: 8 October 2026

1. Who is responsible

The data controller for this service is David Larsson, a private individual in Sweden. You can reach me at info@disco.games.

Guess the Career is a football career guessing game available at guessthecareer.disco.games. This policy explains what personal data is processed when you use it.

3. What is public

The leaderboard is visible to everyone who visits the site. For signed-in users it shows your display name, country flag, score and streak badge. Clicking a name also shows that person's three best leagues as percentages. Guest accounts are not shown on the leaderboard. Choose a display name you are comfortable sharing publicly. You can change it any time in your profile.

5. Transfers outside the EU/EEA

Some providers above are based in, or may process data in, countries outside the EU/EEA, such as the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask us for more information at info@disco.games.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Accounts, profiles and gameplay data: until you delete your account.
  • Guest accounts: deleted automatically after 30 days without activity.
  • Backups: if you delete your account, copies in backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Technical logs: kept by our infrastructure providers according to their own retention periods.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get access to the personal data we hold about you
  • have incorrect data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interest
  • receive your data in a portable format

Delete your account yourself: sign in, open your profile and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account, progress, wrong guesses and leaderboard entry. It cannot be undone.

For anything else, such as access to your data, removing your country flag or other requests, email info@disco.games. We aim to reply within one month.

If you think we handle your data incorrectly, you also have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY) at imy.se.

8. Cookies and local storage

The site stores your sign-in session in your browser's local storage. It is strictly necessary for you to stay signed in and keep your progress, and it is removed when you sign out or clear your browser data. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, Google may set its own cookies on its sign-in pages, governed by Google's policies.

9. Security

Answers are checked on the server, access to the database is restricted with row level security and column permissions, and data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Children

The service is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service changes, for example when we add features. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. For significant changes, we will make it visible in the service.
