3. What is public

Leaderboards are visible to everyone who visits the games. Guest accounts are never shown on a leaderboard. For signed-in players, the following is shown:

  • Guess the Career: your display name, country flag, score and streak badge. Clicking a name also shows that person's three best leagues as percentages.
  • Mumla: your display name, country flag, and how many words you have solved out of the days you have played. Clicking a name also shows how many words that person solved on each attempt.

Challenges in Mumla are only visible to the two players involved. After both of you have played, each of you can see the other's word, number of attempts and the result, and your head-to-head record against each other.

Choose a display name you are comfortable sharing publicly. You can change it at any time in your profile.

Some providers above are based in, or may process data in, countries outside the EU/EEA, such as the United States. Where that happens, the transfer relies on safeguards required by the GDPR, such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. You can ask for more information at info@disco.games.

6. How long we keep your data

  • Accounts, profiles, gameplay data and challenges: until you delete your account. Challenges you took part in are deleted together with your account.
  • Guest accounts: deleted automatically after 30 days without activity.
  • Backups: if you delete your account, copies in backups are removed within 30 days.
  • Technical logs: kept by our infrastructure providers according to their own retention periods.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • get access to the personal data we hold about you
  • have incorrect data corrected
  • have your data erased
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interest
  • receive your data in a portable format

Delete your account yourself: sign in to the game, open your profile and choose Delete account (in Mumla: Radera konto). This permanently removes your account and everything connected to it in that game, including progress and your leaderboard entry. It cannot be undone. Since each game has its own account, delete it in each game you have used.

For anything else, such as access to your data, removing your country flag or other requests, email info@disco.games. We aim to reply within one month.

If you think we handle your data incorrectly, you have the right to complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten, IMY) at imy.se.

8. Cookies and local storage

The games store your sign-in session in your browser's local storage. This is strictly necessary for you to stay signed in and keep your progress. Mumla also stores your game for the day, your colour settings and which challenge notifications you have seen, so the game works the way you left it. This data stays in your browser and is removed when you sign out or clear your browser data.

The Cloudflare Turnstile check in Guess the Career is used only for security, not for advertising or tracking. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you sign in, Google may set its own cookies on its sign-in pages, governed by Google's policies.

9. Security

Answers are checked on the server, so they are not sent to your browser before you have played. Access to the databases is restricted with row level security, and data is sent over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data.

10. Children

The games are not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the games change, for example when we add a game or a feature. The date at the top shows when it was last updated. For significant changes, we will make it visible in the games.
